About us

Made for the people who live here

Bhilai Connect started with a simple observation: finding reliable, up-to-date information about your own city shouldn't take five different apps and a dozen phone calls.

We're building a single, trustworthy home for everyday local information — from where to eat tonight, to what petrol costs this morning, to which businesses in your area are actually verified.

Our mission is to make Bhilai, Durg and the surrounding areas easier to navigate and discover, for residents who've lived here for decades and newcomers finding their way around.
