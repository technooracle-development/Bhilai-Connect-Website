Account & Privacy

Delete Your Bhilai Connect Account

Manage your account and personal data with complete transparency.

If you wish to permanently delete your Bhilai Connect account and associated data, you can do so directly from within the mobile app. Follow the steps below, or contact us if you're unable to sign in.

In-app steps

Delete your account in the app

The process takes less than a minute from within Bhilai Connect.

1

Open Bhilai Connect

Launch the app on your device.

2

Sign in to your account

Log in with the credentials linked to your account.

3

Go to Profile

Tap on your profile icon from the bottom navigation.

4

Open Settings

Find Settings from your Profile screen.

5

Open Danger Zone

Scroll to the Danger Zone section within Settings.

6

Tap Delete Account

Select the Delete Account option.

7

Confirm the deletion request

Confirm when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted.

Transparency

What data will be deleted

Once confirmed, the following is permanently removed from our systems.

Deleted upon confirmation

Account
Profile information
Profile photo
Saved / favorite places
User preferences
Authentication information linked to your account

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period if required for:

  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Legal obligations

If retained, the data is stored only for the minimum period required by applicable laws or security requirements.
